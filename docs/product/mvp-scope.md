# MVP scope and sequencing

## Release A: trustworthy price board

### Included

- Canonical catalog for four initial coconut products.
- Pollachi-area markets and geography.
- AGMARKNET/data.gov.in adapter.
- Immutable raw-record store and normalization.
- Operator/contributor quote entry.
- Validation, anomaly review and evidence-backed aggregation.
- Public Tamil/English price pages and history.
- Price alerts and source-health console.

### Not included

- User listings or offers.
- Payments, logistics or disputes.

### Exit gate

- Three independent verified sources for every actively published local bucket.
- Source observation time visible on all public numbers.
- No unresolved critical provenance defects for four consecutive weeks.
- Returning-user evidence that prices solve a real acquisition need.

## Release B: assisted leads

### Included

- User/organization profiles and granular verification.
- Sell lots and buy requirements.
- Explainable matching.
- Controlled contact reveal and operations assistance.

### Exit gate

- At least 70% of active requirements receive a qualified match within 24 hours.
- Stale-listing rate is controlled through expiry/reconfirmation.
- Abuse and privacy controls pass review.

## Release C: structured deals

### Included

- Offers/counter-offers.
- Deal Card and revisions.
- Pickup, delivery, acceptance and direct-payment confirmation.
- Evidence and disputes.

### Exit gate

- At least 30% buyer repeat rate within 60 days.
- Fewer than 5% unresolved disputes among completed deals.
- Every completed trade has an auditable accepted Deal Card.

## Release D: partner fulfillment

### Included

- Inspection partner workflow.
- Logistics quote/referral.
- FPO aggregation and settlement export.

### Exit gate

- Partner service levels are measurable.
- Unit economics remain positive after operations effort.
- Legal review covers the final operating model.

## Scope-control rule

A feature moves earlier only if it is required to validate price trust or marketplace liquidity. Attractive but non-essential capabilities—AI grading, credit, wallets, statewide logistics and consumer delivery—do not override this rule.
