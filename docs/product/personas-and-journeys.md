# Personas and journeys

## 1. Small or medium farmer

### Goals

- Know whether a local quote is fair.
- Find a credible buyer without repeatedly calling many contacts.
- Understand deductions before agreeing.
- Receive payment on the promised timeline.

### Constraints

- May use an older Android phone and intermittent data.
- May prefer Tamil voice communication.
- Quantity may be approximate before harvest.
- May rely on a trader for harvest crew, dehusking, aggregation or working capital.

### Core journey

1. Opens a shareable local price page.
2. Selects mature coconut, per-nut, farm-gate, Pollachi area.
3. Sees a recent range, sources and freshness.
4. Signs in with OTP and creates a lot, optionally by voice-assisted operator flow.
5. Receives structured offers and compares net terms.
6. Accepts one offer and sees the Deal Card in Tamil.
7. Records pickup/count and later confirms payment.

### Failure recovery

- Offline draft retained when connection drops.
- Operator can complete a draft after explicit farmer confirmation.
- A missed pickup can be rescheduled or cancelled with a recorded reason.

## 2. FPO or cooperative manager

### Goals

- Aggregate member supply into a marketable volume.
- Preserve member-level ownership and allocation.
- Compare buyer terms consistently.
- Produce transparent settlement records.

### Journey

1. Creates an organization and completes applicable verification.
2. Invites staff with limited roles.
3. Records member lots or imports a reviewed template.
4. Publishes an aggregated lot.
5. Compares offers by net realization and fulfilment terms.
6. Records weighment and member allocation.
7. Exports a settlement statement.

## 3. Local aggregator or trader

### Goals

- Discover supply quickly.
- Coordinate repeated routes and loading.
- Maintain a reputation with sellers and buyers.
- Avoid stock/quality surprises.

### Journey

1. Registers an organization and verifies applicable documents.
2. Saves sourcing areas and product preferences.
3. Receives candidate lots and publishes recurring demand.
4. Makes time-limited offers with transport responsibility.
5. Uses repeat-deal shortcuts for known sellers.

## 4. Processor

### Goals

- Maintain predictable input quality and volume.
- Source by moisture/oil/defect specifications.
- Schedule deliveries and retain inspection evidence.

### Journey

1. Defines a reusable milling-copra requirement template.
2. Publishes weekly dated requirements.
3. Reviews matched lots and assay/inspection evidence.
4. Agrees weighment, acceptance and payment rules.
5. Records delivered quantity and any documented deduction.

## 5. Institutional or city buyer

### Goals

- Obtain dependable count/size and delivery.
- Receive business documentation and invoices where applicable.
- Repeat orders without renegotiating every field.

### Journey

1. Creates a delivery-based requirement.
2. Reviews verified suppliers and landed-price offers.
3. Accepts a deal with delivery and acceptance window.
4. Confirms delivery or raises a structured discrepancy.
5. Repeats the requirement with an adjusted date/quantity.

## 6. Price contributor

### Goals

- Submit a current market quote quickly.
- Correct an honest mistake transparently.
- Understand how the quote contributes without exposing private identity.

### Journey

1. Opens the contributor form for their approved products/markets.
2. Enters product, grade, unit, stage, price/range, quantity and validity.
3. Confirms inclusions/exclusions.
4. Receives acceptance, review or rejection state.
5. Submits a correction that preserves the original.

## 7. Market operator

### Goals

- Keep price pages fresh and defensible.
- Resolve mapping/anomaly queues efficiently.
- Assist real users without impersonating them.

### Journey

1. Reviews source-health dashboard.
2. Resolves unmapped markets/products.
3. Investigates anomalous observations using raw provenance.
4. Approves/rejects with a reason.
5. Contacts contributors for clarification through recorded operations notes.
