# Domain glossary

## Commercial terms

**Available quantity**  
The amount a seller declares can be supplied during the lot’s availability window. It is not automatically stock-verified.

**Ball copra**  
Copra dried inside the shell and typically used for edible or religious purposes. It must not share a price bucket with milling copra.

**Buy requirement**  
A structured statement of demand from a buyer, including product, grade, quantity, delivery/pickup area, required date and commercial constraints.

**Completed trade price**  
The final price recorded for a deal that reached completion and passed applicable verification checks. It is higher-quality evidence than an unaccepted quote but can still contain reporting error.

**Deal Card**  
The immutable commercial snapshot created when an offer is accepted. It records product, grade, quantity, price, unit, location, costs, payment and acceptance terms.

**Delivered price**  
A price that includes transport to the agreed destination. It must not be compared directly with an ex-farm price without normalizing included costs.

**Executable quote**  
A price offered by an identified participant for a specified quantity, grade, location and validity period. It is stronger than general market commentary.

**Ex-farm price**  
The price at the farm before transport and any explicitly excluded harvesting, dehusking or loading costs.

**Fair Average Quality (FAQ)**  
A recognized quality designation used in agricultural pricing and procurement. The exact parameters must be tied to the relevant commodity specification.

**Farm-gate price**  
The price received or offered at the production location. Included services and deductions still need to be stated.

**Grade**  
A named or measured quality category. LiveCoco stores structured attributes behind the display label so incompatible grades are not silently averaged.

**Market stage**  
The point in the value chain represented by a price: farm-gate, regulated market, wholesale, processor purchase or retail.

**Mature coconut**  
A fully developed coconut sold for culinary use, processing or conversion. It is distinct from tender coconut and copra.

**Milling copra**  
Dried coconut kernel primarily intended for oil extraction.

**Modal price**  
The most commonly reported/traded price in a mandi dataset. It is not necessarily an immediately available purchase offer.

**Net realization**  
The seller’s proceeds after commissions, harvest, dehusking, loading, transport, quality deductions and other agreed costs.

**Offer**  
An immutable commercial proposal with an expiry. A counter-offer creates a new revision rather than mutating the original.

**Price observation**  
One source-specific statement of price with product, market, unit, stage, time and provenance.

**Sell lot**  
A time-bounded statement that produce controlled by a seller or authorized agent is available under stated conditions.

**Tender coconut**  
An immature coconut harvested for drinking water and tender meat. Prices are commonly count-based and highly sensitive to size, age and delivery timing.

## Data-quality terms

**Confidence**  
A categorical expression of evidence quality derived from freshness, source diversity, verification and dispersion. It is not a probability that the price is correct.

**Contributor**  
An approved person or organization that submits structured market quotes.

**Freshness**  
Elapsed time since a price was observed in the market, not merely since LiveCoco retrieved it.

**Normalization**  
Mapping raw source values into canonical product, grade, market, unit and timestamp fields while preserving the original record.

**Price aggregate**  
A derived low/high/reference range computed only from compatible eligible observations.

**Provenance**  
The trace from a displayed price to source, raw record, normalization version, validation result and correction history.

**Source diversity**  
The number of independent organizations represented in evidence. Multiple submissions from one organization do not count as independent sources.

## Trust terms

**Business-document verified**  
Specific applicable business documents were checked. This does not prove stock, quality or good conduct.

**Identity verified**  
Evidence was reviewed to connect a person to a claimed identity. It is distinct from mobile verification.

**Mobile verified**  
The user proved control of a phone number through OTP.

**Stock verified**  
A specific lot was independently checked within a defined time window. This claim expires and does not transfer to other lots.

**Verified source**  
A price contributor whose identity, organization and market role have been reviewed and approved.
